# OIBSIP Task 1 – Exploratory Data Analysis on Retail Sales Data

## Internship
Oasis Infobyte – Data Analytics Internship

## Objective
The objective of this project is to analyze retail sales data and identify
sales trends, high-performing products, revenue patterns, promotional effects,
and relationships between numerical variables.

## Dataset
The dataset contains retail sales information including:

- Store ID
- Item ID
- Sales
- Price
- Promotion
- Weekday
- Month
- Year
- Day

The dataset contains 3,767,338 records covering the period from 2019 to 2023.

## Tools and Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Google Colab

## Analysis Performed

1. Dataset inspection
2. Data quality checking
3. Descriptive statistics
4. Monthly sales trend analysis
5. Quarterly sales analysis
6. Top 10 products by sales
7. Revenue analysis
8. Promotion analysis
9. Correlation analysis
10. Business insights and recommendations

## Key Findings

- The highest-selling month was March 2023 with 2,629,132 units.
- Q2 2023 recorded the highest quarterly sales with 7,186,931 units.
- Item 20 was the top product by units sold.
- Item 20 also generated the highest revenue.
- The dataset contains 42 stores and 50 items.
- Total units sold were 109,778,526.
- Calculated total revenue was approximately ₹5.84 billion.

## Business Recommendations

### 1. Improve Inventory Planning
Prepare inventory and staffing for periods with consistently higher sales,
particularly Q1 and Q2.

### 2. Prioritize High-Performing Products
Monitor inventory levels of products that consistently generate high sales
and revenue, particularly Item 20.

### 3. Use Promotions Strategically
Compare promotional and non-promotional periods and focus promotions on
products and periods where they produce measurable improvements in sales.

## Conclusion

The analysis identified important patterns in retail sales from 2019 to 2023.
The results can support inventory planning, product prioritization, and
data-driven promotional strategies.
